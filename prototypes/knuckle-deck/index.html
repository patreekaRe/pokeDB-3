How to play
  • Tap three cards to queue them, then hit Fight! Both queues play out one step at a time.
  • Attack against attack: the higher SPD hits. Same speed means both hit (a trade).
  • Stand Block stops High and Overhead. Crouch Block stops High and Low.
  • Landing a hit staggers the opponent for the next step. Their card for that step is lost and your next attack lands for free. That's a combo.
  • Link: +2 damage in a combo. Ender: +2 for every earlier hit in the combo, then knocks down. Launcher: knocks the foe airborne, and only Air moves need that.
  • Unsafe attacks leave you staggered next step if they're blocked or whiff.
  • Each turn the enemy shows one tell. Learn each enemy's habits from the scouting report.
  • Stagger and combos reset between turns. Unused cards stay in your hand.
