How to play

  1. Pick a starter. Its type decides which attack cards you can use.
  2. Build a deck of 5–10 cards (up to 3 copies of each). Tap or drag cards in.
  3. Battle! Each turn you draw 5 cards and have 3 ⚡ energy. Tap a card to play it. Its cost is the number in the corner.
  4. Watch the enemy's intent above its head. It tells you what it will do next turn, so you can block or hurry.
  5. End Turn when you're done. The enemy acts, then you draw a fresh hand.
  6. Win to build a streak. Each win unlocks new cards and, later, new starters.

Good to know

  • 🔥 > 🌿 > 💧 > 🔥 Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water, Water beats Fire: ×1.5 damage. The other way round is ×0.5.
  • 🛡️ Block soaks up damage. It disappears at the start of your next turn.
  • 🔥 Burn hurts the enemy at the start of its turn, then wears off by 1 each turn.
  • 🎯 Focus adds bonus damage to your next attack.
  • 💨 Weaken makes the enemy's next attack deal half damage.
