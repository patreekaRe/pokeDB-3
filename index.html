How to play

  1. Pick a starter. Each one has its own fixed 10-card deck. Check it out before you begin.
  2. Climb the map. Start at the bottom and pick a path upward: ⚔️ fights, 💀 elites, 🏥 rest sites and 🎁 treasure. Your HP carries over between fights.
  3. Battle. Each turn you draw 5 cards and have 3 ⚡ energy. Tap a card to play it. Its cost is the number in the corner. Press End Turn when you're done.
  4. Read the enemy's intent above its head. It shows what it will do next turn, so you can block or hurry.
  5. Grow your deck. After fights, pick 1 of 3 new moves. Elites, bosses and treasure give relics, held items with permanent bonuses.
  6. Beat the boss at the top of each of the 3 biomes. After the first two bosses your Pokémon evolves: more HP, a full heal and stronger moves.

Good to know

  • 🔥 > 🌿 > 💧 > 🔥 Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water, Water beats Fire: ×1.5 damage. The other way round is ×0.5. This works both ways: enemy attacks use their own type against you. A ▲ next to an enemy's attack means it is strong against you, a ▼ means it is weak. Neutral enemies are always ×1.
  • 🛡️ Block soaks up damage. It disappears at the start of your next turn.
  • 🔥 Burn hurts the enemy at the start of its turn, then wears off by 1 each turn.
  • 🎯 Focus adds bonus damage to your next attack.
  • 💨 Weaken makes the enemy's next attack deal half damage.
  • 🔒 Locked starters unlock through achievements. Tap one to see what to do.
  • If you faint, the run is over. Your unlocked starters and stats are kept.
