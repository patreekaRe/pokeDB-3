    How to play

    1. Pick a starter and preview its deck. It's fixed - no editing.
    2. Climb the map: ⚔️ fights, 💀 elites, 🏥 rest sites, 🎁 treasure, then 👑 a boss.
    3. In battle: draw 5 cards, get 3⚡ energy, tap a card to play it. End Turn when done.
    4. Win fights to add moves to your deck and earn 💰 PokéCoins. Evolve after each biome's boss.
    5. Beat all 3 bosses to win the run.

    Good to know

    • 🔥>🌿>💧>🔥 type advantage: +30% / -25% damage, both ways. Watch for ▲▼ on enemy attacks.
    • 🛡️Block absorbs damage this round · 🔥Burn ticks each enemy turn · 🎯Focus boosts your next hit · 💨Weaken halves their next hit.
    • 😡 Enrage: fights get harder after turn 6, so don't stall.
    • ⭐ Trainer Levels and 🔒 locked starters unlock through the 🛒 Shop or achievements. If you faint, your unlocks and coins are kept.
