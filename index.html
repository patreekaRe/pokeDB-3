    How to play

    🏆 Your run

    Four steps from starter to champion

    1. Pick a starterFire, Grass or Water: each type plays differently.
    2. 🗺️Climb the mapChoose your path, room by room, up to the boss.
    3. ⚔️Win battlesEach win teaches you a new move and pays 💰.
    4. Beat 3 bossesYour Pokémon evolves after each one. Beat the third to win.

    ⚔️ Your turn

    Play your cards, then it's the enemy's move

    1. PP3/3 Get 3 PPYour energy. It refills every turn.
    2. 🔥🌿💧 Play cardsEach one costs the PP in its corner.
    3. End Turn End your turnThe enemy moves, then you draw 5 new cards.

    ⚔️12TackleThe enemy's next move, over its head. Block before a big hit!

    Your Bag holds your deck, relics, items and the map key.

    🧭 The map

    Start at the bottom and choose your path to the boss

    ⚔️FightA wild Pokémon. Win a new card, sometimes an item.
    💀EliteMuch tougher, but drops a relic.
    🏥Pokémon CenterRest to heal, or have Chansey PP Up a move.
    🎁TreasurePick a free relic.
    🏪Poké MartSpend prize money (💴) on cards, items, relics or forgetting a move.
    ❓Mystery eventSomething happens. Choose what to do: most rewards have a cost.
    👹BossBeat it to evolve and move on.

    🔥 Types and statuses

    Hit wild Pokémon's weakness and keep an eye on the badges
