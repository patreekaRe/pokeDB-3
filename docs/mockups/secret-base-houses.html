Secret Base House Catalogue

The Ancient Tree's lot in the Clearing gets a real house. Every base starts in a Starter Cottage. Players buy more from a catalogue on the PC, earn the best ones through badges and feats, and repaint whichever one they live in.

On the Clearing's PC

A HOUSE CATALOGUE page next to HOUSE UPGRADES. Tap a house to see it on your lot. The example save has 3,240 PokéCoins, the Log Cabin bought, and the tree kept from before.

How it would work

Only the outside changes

Rooms, furniture, residents and the backyard stay as they are. A house swaps the model on the lot and the doorway you walk through, and brings a matching wallpaper and floor for free.

A first change anyone can afford

Every house has three paint jobs. Repainting costs 250 PokéCoins, so a new player can change their base's look the same day they buy it. Homestead houses at 1,500 to 2,000 are the first real move.

Earned houses can't be bought

Gym Hall, Ryokan, Ranger Lodge, Sky Spire, Poké Ball Dome and Crystal Palace each come from something players already chase. Locked rows say exactly what to do.

Friends see it (new)

Today Visit drops a friend straight inside. With houses, Visit starts on your front lawn: your house, your buddy and a mailbox with your name, then a tap on the door goes in. Your Trainers page and Plaza card show a small picture of it too.

Old saves are safe

Anyone who owned the base before this keeps living in the Ancient Tree, with the Starter Cottage given to them free. New players can earn the tree with the Clearing Badge.

Built like the towns

Each house is a real 3D model, built the way the Nietos' cottage and the town manors are. Only the one you live in is ever built, so the Clearing costs the phone no more than it does now.
