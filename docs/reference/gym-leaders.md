# Gym Leaders: who they are

The Gym Leaders are the user's real friends. The user gave each one's personality in chat (2026-10-13) and that chat is
gone; this page is rebuilt from the comments and speeches in `js/data/gym.js` and their homes in `js/data/towns.js`.
Read it before writing a new line for any of them, and keep their voice: short lines, one joke a line, `{boss}` /
`{badge}` / `{trainer}` placeholders. Anything new the user says about a friend goes here too, in their words where
possible. Since 2026-10-14 every leader has their own `poster`, `gold`, `visit` and `posterSeen` lines (the generic ones
in `leader()` are only a fallback); `visit` lines lose their `LAW:` / `JONA:` tags in the Secret Base
(`leaderLines()` in `js/base-3d.js`), so the Nietos' and Phil's are written without them. The mechanics (talks, badges, posters, visits) are in CLAUDE.md's Gym Leaders note.

## Who knows whom

They're one friend group. Most of them play **Valorant** (and League's **ARAM**) together at night, in voice chat or on
Discord; "the boys" is that group. Law and Britt are married with a baby, Linc. Law's lines name Phil, CJ (Clarence),
Jahmes and Ricky as his boyfriends, and John as someone who'd be proud of him; Pat roasts Law in voice chat; Clarence
names Shaun as one of the boys who watches his show. Keep cross-references like these: the user enjoys them.

## The Gym Leaders app (2026-10-14)

The Pokédex device's Gym Leaders app (`js/gymdex.js`): met (`save.seen.gym`, set as their fight begins), beaten
(`save.gym`), lost to (`save.gymLost`), and researched at `GYM_GOLD` wins. Each leader's one-line Pokédex entry is
`GYM_BIO` in `js/data/gym.js`, shown once beaten: a new leader needs a `GYM_BIO` line too, in the voice above.
Saves from before the app get every Leader they had met, beaten or lost to marked met and new once on load
(`gymAppNews()` in `js/storage.js`, flagged by `save.gymAppNews`), so the "!" reaches them too.

- **Kenmatta's page** (2026-10-18, the user's ask: "can Ken get his own section"): a last banner, Kenmatta's Dojo
  (`dojoGroup()` / `kenSpec` in `js/gymdex.js`, his kombat arena painted on it: `still()` in `js/pokedex.js` takes a
  `PLACE_ART` id through `isPlace()`). Met is `seen.gym` 'ken' (set as a run's fight against him begins), a win, or
  `save.dojo.met`; beaten is `kenWinsOf() >= 1`; researched (the gold slot) the Black Belt. His record: wins, losses
  (`save.kenLost`, counted in `afterFight()`), belt, stars, the next belt's needs; his sheet a belt track instead of the
  rematch rewards, the Dojo or Black Belt badge. The app's opener counts leaders only, but opens once he's met too.
- **The crest's badge is in full colour before it's won** (2026-10-14, the user's ask: "I want the players to see what
  they're getting"). It used to be a dark seal till earned; now only the extra shine (metalness, glow) waits on the win
  (`crestArt()` / `gymCrest()` in `js/event-3d.js`).
